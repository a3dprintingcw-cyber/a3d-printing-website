Materials

Pick the right material

Not sure what you need? Tell us what the part is for in the quote form and we'll recommend one.

MaterialBest forFeelNotes
PLAModels, decor, prototypes, giftsRigid, matte or glossyOur most popular — great detail, many colors
PETGFunctional parts, outdoor useTougher, slightly flexibleBetter heat & impact resistance than PLA
TPUPhone cases, gaskets, gripsRubber-like, flexibleBendable and durable
ABSMechanical & automotive partsRigid, heat-resistantGood strength for functional components
ASAOutdoor signs & parts that live in the sunRigid, weather-resistantUV-stable — holds colour where PLA and PETG fade
Resin (SLA)Miniatures, jewelry, fine detailSmooth, high detailBest surface finish, small parts
